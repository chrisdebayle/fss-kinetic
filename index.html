The three frameworks on the rail
Stage 01 · Signal
RVP

The Referential Value Proposition. I open by naming the buyer’s operational reality rather than pitching, so the response is “that’s exactly us,” not a defence reflex. This is what turns dials into conversations.

Stage 02 · Qualified pipeline
Disposition Science

Every completed conversation is tagged. The distribution names which lever (list, message, rep, follow-up, timing) is constraining the funnel, so corrections mid-Sprint are evidence-led rather than guesswork.

Stage 03 · Maturation
VALID™

Five wins (Validated, Aligned, Locked, Invested, Documented), each with an owner and a required artifact. Only documented is forecastable. My own model, trademark pending.

A positive meeting is not a win. Wins are earned in provable increments, and trust is the system that produces them.
Five documented wins and one assumed win is not 83% qualified. It is unforecastable.

The Engine

How 4 to 8 gets built, not hoped for
Creating relevance where there is no brand recognition
Angle 01
Vertical problem patterns

The operational problems specific to manufacturing, logistics, and construction that AI genuinely remedies when it is architected properly. I open on the problem, never on the product.

Angle 02
Modernization signals

Legacy ERP gaps, manual handoffs between systems, scheduling that lives in one person’s head. Visible from the outside, expensive on the inside, and already costing them money.

Angle 03
AI-intent signals

Companies already looking: relevant job postings, pilot announcements, new data or operations leadership. Budget attention already exists, and often a disappointing pilot to compare against.

This is targeting as research, not list buying. Every account earns an angle before it earns a call, which is what makes a cold conversation land when the logo means nothing yet.
Mind share is not built in one channel
Channel 01 · Volume
Researched outbound

Dials, sequenced email, and targeted LinkedIn against accounts that already carry an angle. This is the engine that runs every week regardless of anything else.

Channel 02 · Trust
In-person drop-ins

DFW is my home market, so a drop-in is a drive, not a flight. Reserved for accounts where the angle has landed but the relationship needs a face.

Channel 03 · Cut-through
Direct mail

A physical touch survives a full inbox. Tested as a measured wave against Tier-1 accounts, then kept or killed on the disposition data.

Channel 04 · Compounding
Network and association

DFW vertical associations and trade events, where the same rooms hold the same operators repeatedly. Slow to start, then self-reinforcing.

We are not targeting manufacturing exclusively, but it sets the tone for the mix. It is a relationship-first, referral-driven culture where a face and a handshake outrun a sequence, and a plan that ignores that will stall in month two.
